# Retail — Vendor Contract Terms: Marketing (coding-relevant extracts)

Version: 2.0.0
Effective from: 2026-01-01

Extracts from active marketing and advertising agreements. These are `doc_type`
vendor_contract, included in retrieval when a vendor-specific coding or split
term matters.

## 1. ReachAds — media-buying agreement

ReachAds buys paid media on the company's behalf under a media-buying agreement.
All media spend codes to 6600 (Marketing and Advertising). Where ReachAds bills
a separate management fee, it is also 6600, because it is part of delivering the
promotion.

## 2. BrightWorks Studio — creative services agreement

BrightWorks Studio provides campaign creative under a services agreement. Billed
as "design services", the deliverable is promotional creative, so it codes to
6600 (Marketing and Advertising), not 6500. The contract makes clear the purpose
is campaign promotion.

## 3. BoothBuild — event fabrication agreement

BoothBuild fabricates trade-show booths under an event-production agreement.
Although BoothBuild is a fabrication contractor, the spend is promotional and
codes to 6600. The contract ties the work to specific trade-show events.

## 4. MarketOne — integrated agency agreement

MarketOne provides both media buying and a marketing-automation platform under
one agreement. Media spend codes to 6600 and the platform subscription codes to
7200. MarketOne itemises the two separately, so a MarketOne invoice is a
split-coding case: media 6600, platform 7200.

## 5. PressRelations — PR retainer agreement

PressRelations provides public-relations services under a monthly retainer. The
retainer codes to 6600 (Marketing and Advertising) as a promotional service.
