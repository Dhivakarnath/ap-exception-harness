# Retail — Vendor Contract Terms (coding-relevant extracts)

Version: 2.0.0
Effective from: 2026-01-01

Extracts from active vendor master service agreements, retained because a
contract sometimes fixes how a vendor's spend is coded or capped. These are a
distinct `doc_type` (vendor_contract) so a caller can scope retrieval to
policy and precedent only, or include contracts when a vendor-specific term
matters.

## 1. Initech Services — MSA rate card

Initech Services (V-1004) bills under a master service agreement at a fixed
monthly retainer plus time-and-materials overage. All Initech spend under this
MSA is professional services and codes to 6500. The MSA caps monthly billing;
an Initech invoice materially above the cap is a contract exception and should
be routed to a human even though the account (6500) is clear.

## 2. Analytics Platform — enterprise licence

The analytics-platform enterprise licence is an annual subscription coded to
7200 (Software Subscriptions). The contract bundles a fixed number of onboarding
hours at no separate charge; because those hours are not separately invoiced,
the whole renewal codes to 7200 with no split (consistent with policy clause 3).

## 3. Facilities Contractor — cleaning services

The facilities cleaning contractor provides recurring janitorial service under
contract. Contracted cleaning labour codes to 6500 (Professional Services) per
policy clause 11; the consumable supplies the contractor separately bills code
to 6410 (Office Supplies). A single invoice mixing labour and supplies is a
split-coding case (policy clause 13).
