# Retail — Vendor Contract Terms: Professional Services (coding-relevant extracts)

Version: 2.0.0
Effective from: 2026-01-01

Extracts from active professional-services agreements. These are `doc_type`
vendor_contract, included in retrieval when a vendor-specific coding or cap term
matters.

## 1. Blackwood LLP — legal services agreement

Blackwood LLP provides outside-counsel services under a retainer-plus-matter
agreement. All Blackwood spend codes to 6500 (Professional Services). Matter-based
fees and the monthly retainer are both 6500. A regulatory filing fee Blackwood
pays on the company's behalf and passes through is 6500 as part of the legal
service; a direct government payment is not.

## 2. Meridian Audit — audit engagement letter

Meridian Audit provides annual audit and tax-preparation services under an
engagement letter. All Meridian spend codes to 6500. The engagement fee is fixed
annually; overage for extra scope is a contract exception routed to a human even
though the account (6500) is clear.

## 3. TalentBridge — recruitment agreement

TalentBridge provides recruitment services on a contingency basis. Placement
fees code to 6500 (Professional Services). The contract sets a placement-fee
percentage; an invoice deviating materially from the agreed percentage is a
contract exception routed to a human.

## 4. CleanCo — facilities services agreement

CleanCo provides recurring cleaning under a facilities-services contract.
Contracted cleaning *labour* codes to 6500 (Professional Services); *consumable
supplies* CleanCo separately bills code to 6410 (Office Supplies). A single
invoice mixing labour and supplies is a split-coding case.

## 5. Summit Advisory — consulting master agreement

Summit Advisory provides management consulting under a master agreement. All
Summit engagement fees code to 6500. Where a Summit statement of work includes a
software tool sublicensed to the company and separately invoiced, that software
line splits to 7200.
