# Retail — Coding Guide: Marketing and Advertising (Account 6600)

Version: 2.0.0
Effective from: 2026-01-01

This guide covers coding of promotional spend to account 6600 (Marketing and
Advertising). The defining test for 6600 is purpose: if the spend exists to
promote the business, it is 6600 — even when the work is a "service" (which
tempts 6500) or a "print run" (which tempts 6410). Every clause resolves to 6600
unless it redirects.

## 1. Media buys and paid advertising

Account 6600 — Marketing and Advertising.

Paid search, paid social, programmatic display, retargeting, print advertising,
broadcast and radio spots, and out-of-home placements are coded to 6600. A media
invoice from an advertising platform is 6600, not 7200 — the platform is selling
ad placement, not a software subscription, even though it bills through software.

## 2. Creative and campaign production

Account 6600 — Marketing and Advertising.

Campaign concepting, creative production, copywriting, photography, video
production, and design work whose purpose is a marketing campaign are coded to
6600. This is 6600 even when billed as "design services" or "creative services",
because the purpose is promotion. Do not code promotional creative to 6500.

## 3. Promotional materials and branded merchandise

Account 6600 — Marketing and Advertising.

Branded merchandise, giveaways, promotional packaging, branded apparel, and
swag are coded to 6600. Branded print — catalogues, brochures, flyers, posters,
event signage — is 6600, in contrast to plain internal printing, which is 6410.

## 4. Trade shows, events, and sponsorship

Account 6600 — Marketing and Advertising.

Trade-show booth production, exhibition-stand build-out, event sponsorship, event
staffing for promotional events, and conference presence are coded to 6600. This
is 6600 even when a construction or fabrication contractor builds the booth,
because the spend is promotional, not a professional-services engagement to run
the business.

## 5. Public relations and influencer fees

Account 6600 — Marketing and Advertising.

Public-relations retainers, media-relations fees, influencer and creator fees,
and affiliate-marketing commissions are coded to 6600. These are promotional
services and belong in 6600 rather than 6500.

## 6. Agency retainers and marketing services

Account 6600 — Marketing and Advertising.

Advertising-agency retainers, marketing-agency fees, SEO and content-marketing
retainers, and social-media management fees are coded to 6600. The agency is
delivering promotion; purpose keeps this in 6600 rather than 6500 even though it
is contracted labour.

## 7. Marketing technology — the 6600/7200 boundary

Split by what is billed.

A marketing *media buy* is 6600. A marketing-technology *software subscription*
— a marketing-automation platform, CRM seat licence, or analytics subscription
used by marketing — is a software subscription coded to 7200. When one vendor
bills both ad spend and a platform subscription on one invoice, split the media
buy to 6600 and the platform subscription to 7200, citing each. Do not lump ad
spend into 7200 or a platform subscription into 6600.

## 8. What does not belong in 6600

Plain internal printing with no promotional purpose is 6410. Professional or
technical labour with no promotional purpose is 6500. A software subscription is
7200. When an invoice sits on the 6600/6500 boundary, apply the purpose test: to
promote (6600) or to run the business (6500). If purpose is genuinely unclear,
abstain and route to a human.
