# Retail — Coding Guide: Computer Supplies (Account 6420)

Version: 2.0.0
Effective from: 2026-01-01

This guide covers coding of computer hardware, peripherals, and accessories to
account 6420 (Computer Supplies). The recurring hazard here is the boundary with
7200 (software) on one side and the capitalisation threshold on the other. Every
clause resolves to 6420 unless it redirects.

## 1. Peripherals and input devices

Account 6420 — Computer Supplies.

Keyboards, mice, trackpads, webcams, headsets, microphones, and presentation
remotes are coded to 6420. A bulk order of peripherals for a team is a single
6420 line. These are hardware accessories, never software, regardless of any
bundled driver or companion app.

## 2. Cables, adapters, and connectivity

Account 6420 — Computer Supplies.

USB cables, HDMI and DisplayPort cables, network patch cables, USB-C hubs,
dongles, and power adapters are coded to 6420. Connectivity accessories are
hardware; do not confuse a network cable with a network *service* or a cloud
*subscription*, which would be 7200.

## 3. Docking stations and stands

Account 6420 — Computer Supplies.

Laptop docking stations, port replicators, monitor arms, laptop stands, and desk
mounts are coded to 6420. A docking station is the classic 6420-vs-7200 trap: it
is hardware (6420) even when purchased from a vendor whose main business is
software, and even when it ships with configuration software.

## 4. Monitors and displays below threshold

Account 6420 — Computer Supplies.

External monitors, portable displays, and small display panels below the
capitalisation threshold are coded to 6420. A large display, video wall, or any
single display at or above the capitalisation threshold is a capital decision
and must be routed to a human.

## 5. Storage and memory below threshold

Account 6420 — Computer Supplies.

External drives, USB flash storage, memory cards, and RAM upgrade modules below
the capitalisation threshold are coded to 6420. Note the contrast with 7200:
*physical* storage media is 6420; *cloud* storage or backup-as-a-service is a
subscription coded to 7200.

## 6. Small networking hardware below threshold

Account 6420 — Computer Supplies.

Desktop switches, Wi-Fi access points, range extenders, and small networking
devices below the capitalisation threshold are coded to 6420. Network *service*
or managed-connectivity *subscriptions* are 7200; internet-access utility bills
are out of scope and route to a human.

## 7. Computers, laptops, and servers at or above threshold

Route to a human — capitalisation decision.

A single computer, laptop, tablet, or server at or above the capitalisation
threshold is a candidate capital asset and must be routed to a human for a
capitalisation decision. Do not expense it to 6420. Only sub-threshold computing
devices are expensed to 6420, and even then only where local policy permits
expensing rather than tracking as a low-value asset.

## 8. What does not belong in 6420

Software, SaaS, cloud subscriptions, and cloud storage are 7200, not 6420.
Printer toner, ink, and paper are consumables coded to 6410, not 6420. Any
capitalisable device at or above threshold routes to a human. When an invoice
mixes hardware and software — a laptop plus a software licence — split the
hardware to 6420 (if sub-threshold) and the software to 7200, citing each.
