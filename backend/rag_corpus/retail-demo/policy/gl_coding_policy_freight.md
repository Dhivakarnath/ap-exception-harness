# Retail — Coding Guide: Inbound Freight and Materials (Accounts 5100 and 5000)

Version: 2.0.0
Effective from: 2026-01-01

This guide covers the two goods-related accounts that appear rarely on non-PO
retail invoices: 5100 (Freight In) and 5000 (Raw Materials). Both are uncommon
here because goods are normally bought against a purchase order that already
carries the account, so a non-PO goods invoice deserves a second look for a
missing PO before coding.

## 1. Inbound freight and carriage

Account 5100 — Freight In.

Inbound freight, carriage-in, courier and delivery charges on purchased goods,
and fuel surcharges and accessorial fees on an inbound shipment are coded to
5100. This is the cost of bringing purchased goods to the business, booked
separately from the goods themselves.

## 2. Customs brokerage and handling on inbound shipments

Account 5100 — Freight In.

Customs-brokerage handling fees and import-handling charges billed by the
freight forwarder on an inbound shipment are coded to 5100. Note the contrast:
the *handling fee* charged by the forwarder is 5100, but *duty or import tax
remitted directly to a government body* is out of scope and routes to a human.

## 3. Bundled goods and freight

Split — 5100 for freight, goods to their own account.

When one invoice lists purchased goods and their inbound freight separately,
split the freight portion to 5100 and code the goods portion to its own account
(5000 for resale/production materials, or the relevant office/computer account
for supplies). Cite the clause for each split line. Do not fold freight into the
goods line or vice versa.

## 4. Direct materials without a PO

Account 5000 — Raw Materials.

On the rare occasion a retail entity buys resale inventory or production
materials without a purchase order, they are coded to 5000. Because this is
uncommon for a retail tenant, a materials invoice with no PO should be
double-checked for a missing PO reference before coding — a missing PO is more
likely than a genuine non-PO materials buy.

## 5. What does not belong in 5100 or 5000

Outbound shipping to customers is not covered by this non-PO policy and routes
to a human. Office consumables are 6410, not 5000. Computer peripherals are
6420. Duty and import tax paid directly to a government body are out of scope.
When a non-PO invoice looks like materials or freight, first confirm there is no
missing PO; if there genuinely is none and the line clearly fits, code it, else
abstain.
