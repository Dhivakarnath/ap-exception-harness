# Retail — Coding Guide: Professional Services (Account 6500)

Version: 2.0.0
Effective from: 2026-01-01

This guide covers coding of external labour and expertise to account 6500
(Professional Services). The recurring hazard is the boundary with 6600
(promotional work performed as a service) and 7200 (services bundled with
software). Every clause resolves to 6500 unless it redirects.

## 1. Consulting and advisory

Account 6500 — Professional Services.

Management consulting, strategy advisory, operational consulting, and subject-
matter advisory engagements are coded to 6500. The deliverable is expertise, not
a good or a licence, so it is 6500 regardless of the consultant's domain.

## 2. Legal fees and outside counsel

Account 6500 — Professional Services.

Law-firm invoices, outside-counsel retainers, litigation support, matter-based
legal fees, patent and trademark agent fees, and notary or commissioning fees
are coded to 6500. A regulatory filing fee charged *through* a professional
adviser is 6500; a statutory fee paid *directly* to a government body is out of
scope and routes to a human.

## 3. Audit, tax, and accounting fees

Account 6500 — Professional Services.

External audit fees, tax-return preparation, statutory accounting, and
outsourced bookkeeping are coded to 6500. Annual audit and year-end tax-prep
invoices from an accounting firm are 6500.

## 4. Contract engineering and staff augmentation

Account 6500 — Professional Services.

Contract engineers, staff-augmentation resources, contract labour, and
outsourced technical work billed by a services vendor are coded to 6500. This is
6500 even when the work is software-related, because the deliverable is *labour*
(hours of a person's expertise), not a software subscription. A software
*licence* the contractor uses is separate and would be 7200 if billed.

## 5. Recruitment and placement fees

Account 6500 — Professional Services.

Recruitment-agency placement fees, contingency fees, executive-search retainers,
and candidate-placement charges are coded to 6500. The service is sourcing
talent; it is professional services.

## 6. Contracted facilities labour

Account 6500 — Professional Services.

Cleaning, maintenance, groundskeeping, pest control, and repair *labour*
performed by a contracted service provider is coded to 6500. This is the labour
on the invoice only. Any consumables the contractor bills separately (cleaning
supplies) are 6410, and any utilities or rent are out of scope. When one invoice
bundles labour and supplies, split them.

## 7. Outsourced operations and BPO

Account 6500 — Professional Services.

Outsourced back-office operations, business-process outsourcing, managed
customer-support labour, and outsourced logistics coordination *labour* are
coded to 6500. The distinction from 7200 is that the deliverable is human labour
or a managed service, not access to a software platform.

## 8. Where 6500 is the wrong account

Promotional or creative work whose purpose is to advertise the business — even
when billed as "design services" or "agency fees" — is 6600, not 6500. Purpose
governs: a design studio producing a campaign is 6600. Software subscriptions
bundled with services stay 7200 unless the services are separately and
materially invoiced. Direct payments to a government body are out of scope. When
an invoice sits on the 6500/6600 line, ask whether the purpose is to *promote*
(6600) or to *deliver expertise/labour to run the business* (6500); if the
purpose is genuinely unclear, abstain.
