# Retail — Coding Guide: Office Supplies (Account 6410)

Version: 2.0.0
Effective from: 2026-01-01

This guide covers day-to-day coding of office-supply invoices to account 6410
(Office Supplies). It exists so that the many small, frequent, low-value
invoices that make up office spend are coded consistently without a human
touching each one. Every clause below resolves to 6410 unless it explicitly
redirects to another account or to a human.

## 1. Paper, stationery, and printed consumables

Account 6410 — Office Supplies.

Copier and printer paper, letterhead stock, envelopes, notebooks, sticky notes,
pens, markers, binders, folders, labels, and staples are coded to 6410. A mixed
stationery order on a single invoice is one 6410 line; there is no need to split
stationery by sub-type. Reams of paper and boxes of toner ordered together stay
on one 6410 line.

## 2. Printer toner, ink, and cartridges

Account 6410 — Office Supplies.

Toner cartridges, inkjet cartridges, drum units, and printer maintenance kits
are consumables coded to 6410, even when the vendor is a computer or electronics
retailer. The distinction from 6420 is function: a cartridge is a consumable
(6410); the printer itself, a cable, or a peripheral device is hardware (6420).

## 3. Breakroom, pantry, and beverage supplies

Account 6410 — Office Supplies.

Coffee, tea, bottled water, soft drinks, snacks, disposable cups, plates,
cutlery, and pantry restocks for staff are coded to 6410. A recurring monthly
pantry-restock subscription from an office-supplies vendor is still 6410 — it is
consumable office supply, not a software subscription, despite the recurring
billing pattern.

## 4. Janitorial and cleaning consumables

Account 6410 — Office Supplies.

Cleaning fluids, paper towels, hand soap, sanitiser, bin liners, and janitorial
consumables are coded to 6410. Note the boundary with 6500: the *consumables*
are 6410, but *contracted cleaning labour* (a cleaning company invoicing for
hours worked) is a professional service coded to 6500. If one invoice bundles
both supplies and labour, split the consumables to 6410 and the labour to 6500.

## 5. Plain internal printing

Account 6410 — Office Supplies.

Internal forms, letterhead, business cards, and plain document printing with no
promotional or campaign purpose are coded to 6410. The moment a print run is
branded, promotional, or part of a marketing campaign — catalogues, brochures,
event signage — it moves to 6600 Marketing and Advertising. Purpose decides the
account, not the fact that both are "printing".

## 6. First-aid, safety, and facility consumables

Account 6410 — Office Supplies.

First-aid kit restocks, safety signage consumables, batteries, light bulbs, and
small facility consumables are coded to 6410. A facility *repair or maintenance
service* performed by a contractor is 6500, not 6410 — the consumable is 6410,
the labour is 6500.

## 7. Small furniture and fixtures below threshold

Account 6410 — Office Supplies.

Low-value office items such as a desk organiser, a single chair, or a small
shelf below the capitalisation threshold are coded to 6410. A furniture purchase
at or above the capitalisation threshold, or a bulk fit-out, is a capital
decision and must be routed to a human — do not expense a capitalisable
furniture project to 6410.

## 8. What does not belong in 6410

The following are not office supplies and must not be coded to 6410: computer
peripherals, cables, and small hardware (6420); software and subscriptions
(7200); promotional or branded print (6600); contracted labour of any kind
(6500); and utilities, rent, or direct statutory payments (out of scope, route
to a human). When an invoice is ambiguous between 6410 and one of these, apply
the chart-of-accounts tie-breakers and, if still unclear, abstain.
