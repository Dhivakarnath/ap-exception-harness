# Retail — Coded-Invoice Precedent: Marketing and Advertising

Version: 2.0.0
Effective from: 2026-01-01

Prior marketing invoices coded and approved by a human, retained as precedent
for account 6600 (Marketing and Advertising) unless noted. Each entry names the
vendor, the billing language, and the confirmed account.

## ReachAds — digital media buy

ReachAds bills for paid search, paid social, and programmatic display. These
have been coded to 6600 (Marketing and Advertising). A "media buy", "paid
search", or "programmatic" line from ReachAds follows this precedent: 6600.

## BrightWorks Studio — campaign creative

BrightWorks Studio bills for campaign creative and video production. Billed as
"design services", the work is promotional and has been coded to 6600, not
6500. A BrightWorks "campaign creative" or "video production" line follows this
precedent: 6600.

## BoothBuild — trade-show stand

BoothBuild, a fabrication contractor, bills for trade-show booth production and
event build-out. Despite being a construction contractor, the spend is
promotional and has been coded to 6600. A "booth production", "exhibition
stand", or "event build" line from BoothBuild follows this precedent: 6600.

## PressRelations — PR retainer

PressRelations bills a monthly public-relations retainer. These have been coded
to 6600. A "PR retainer" or "media relations" line follows this precedent:
6600.

## CreatorNet — influencer fees

CreatorNet bills influencer and affiliate fees. These have been coded to 6600.
An "influencer fee", "creator campaign", or "affiliate commission" line follows
this precedent: 6600.

## PrintPro — branded catalogues

PrintPro bills for branded catalogues and promotional brochures. These have been
coded to 6600 (Marketing and Advertising), not 6410, because the print is
promotional. A "branded catalogue", "brochure run", or "event signage" line
follows this precedent: 6600.

## PrintPro — plain internal forms (coded 6410, not 6600)

On one PrintPro invoice, a plain internal-forms print run with no promotional
purpose was coded to 6410 (Office Supplies), not 6600. A "internal forms" or
"plain letterhead" line follows this precedent: 6410 — purpose decides, not the
print vendor.

## MarketOne — media plus platform (split)

MarketOne billed both a media buy and a marketing-automation platform
subscription on one invoice. The media buy was coded to 6600 and the platform
subscription to 7200. A MarketOne invoice with both follows this precedent:
media 6600, platform 7200.
