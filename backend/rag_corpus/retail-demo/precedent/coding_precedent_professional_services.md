# Retail — Coded-Invoice Precedent: Professional Services

Version: 2.0.0
Effective from: 2026-01-01

Prior professional-services invoices coded and approved by a human, retained as
precedent for account 6500 (Professional Services) unless noted. Each entry
names the vendor, the billing language, and the confirmed account.

## Initech Services — contract engineering

Initech Services (vendor V-1004) bills monthly for contract engineering and
staff-augmentation support. These have consistently been coded to 6500. A
"contract engineering support", "engineering retainer", or "staff augmentation"
line from Initech follows this precedent: 6500.

## Blackwood LLP — outside counsel

Blackwood LLP bills matter-based legal fees and a monthly retainer. These have
been coded to 6500 (Professional Services). A "legal fees", "matter #", or
"counsel retainer" line from Blackwood follows this precedent: 6500.

## Meridian Audit — year-end audit

Meridian Audit bills annual external-audit and tax-preparation fees. These have
been coded to 6500. An "audit fee", "year-end audit", or "tax preparation" line
from Meridian follows this precedent: 6500.

## TalentBridge — recruitment placement

TalentBridge bills contingency placement fees for filled roles. These have been
coded to 6500. A "placement fee", "candidate placement", or "recruitment
retainer" line from TalentBridge follows this precedent: 6500.

## Summit Advisory — management consulting

Summit Advisory bills for management-consulting engagements. These have been
coded to 6500. A "consulting engagement", "advisory fees", or "strategy project"
line from Summit follows this precedent: 6500.

## CleanCo — contracted cleaning labour

CleanCo bills monthly for contracted cleaning labour. The *labour* has been
coded to 6500 (Professional Services). A "cleaning services" or "monthly
cleaning" labour line from CleanCo follows this precedent: 6500. Any separately
billed cleaning *consumables* are 6410.

## FixIt Maintenance — repair labour

FixIt Maintenance bills for facility repair and maintenance labour. These have
been coded to 6500. A "repair labour", "maintenance visit", or "service call"
line from FixIt follows this precedent: 6500.

## BrightWorks Studio — creative work (coded 6600, not 6500)

BrightWorks Studio bills "design services", but the work was campaign creative
whose purpose was promotion. It was coded to 6600 (Marketing and Advertising),
not 6500. A BrightWorks "design services" line for campaign creative follows
this precedent: 6600 — a reminder that purpose, not the word "services",
decides the 6500/6600 boundary.
