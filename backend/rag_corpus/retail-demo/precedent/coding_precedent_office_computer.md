# Retail — Coded-Invoice Precedent: Office and Computer Supplies

Version: 2.0.0
Effective from: 2026-01-01

Prior office- and computer-supply invoices coded and approved by a human,
retained as precedent for accounts 6410 (Office Supplies) and 6420 (Computer
Supplies). Each entry names the vendor, the billing language, and the confirmed
account, with the near-miss account noted where relevant.

## OfficeHub — stationery and paper

OfficeHub bills for paper, stationery, and toner. These have been coded to 6410
(Office Supplies). A "copier paper", "stationery order", or "toner cartridges"
line from OfficeHub follows this precedent: 6410.

## PantryPlus — breakroom restock

PantryPlus bills a recurring monthly pantry restock (coffee, water, snacks).
Despite the recurring billing, these are 6410 (Office Supplies), not 7200. A
"pantry restock", "breakroom consumables", or "monthly beverage" line follows
this precedent: 6410.

## SparkleClean Supplies — janitorial consumables

SparkleClean Supplies bills for cleaning fluids, paper towels, and bin liners.
These are consumables coded to 6410. A "janitorial supplies" or "cleaning
consumables" line follows this precedent: 6410 — distinct from contracted
cleaning *labour*, which is 6500.

## TechGear — peripherals and docking stations

TechGear bills for keyboards, mice, headsets, and docking stations below
threshold. These have been coded to 6420 (Computer Supplies), not 7200. A
"docking stations", "peripheral kit", or "headsets" line from TechGear follows
this precedent: 6420.

## TechGear — external monitors below threshold

TechGear also bills for external monitors under the threshold. These have been
coded to 6420. An "external monitor" line below threshold follows this
precedent: 6420. A single monitor at or above threshold routes to a human.

## CableCo — cables and adapters

CableCo bills for USB-C cables, HDMI cables, and adapters. These have been coded
to 6420. A "cables", "adapters", or "USB hubs" line follows this precedent:
6420.

## TechGear — toner (coded 6410, not 6420)

On one TechGear invoice, a printer-toner line was coded to 6410 (Office
Supplies), not 6420, because toner is a consumable even when sold by a computer
retailer. A "printer toner" line from a computer retailer follows this
precedent: 6410 — the vendor's category does not override the item's nature.

## NetGear Small — desktop switch below threshold

A desktop network switch under threshold from NetGear Small was coded to 6420
(Computer Supplies). A sub-threshold "network switch" or "access point" line
follows this precedent: 6420. Managed-connectivity *subscriptions* are 7200 and
internet-access utility bills route to a human.

## DeskWorks — laptop at threshold (routed to human)

A single laptop at 2,800 from DeskWorks was routed to a human for a
capitalisation decision, not expensed to 6420. An at-or-above-threshold single
computing device follows this precedent: route to a human.

## DeskWorks — bulk keyboards (coded 6420)

Fifty keyboards at 40 each (2,000 total, no single item at threshold) from
DeskWorks were coded to 6420 (Computer Supplies), not routed to a human,
because the threshold is per item. A high-total, low-unit-value hardware line
follows this precedent: 6420.
