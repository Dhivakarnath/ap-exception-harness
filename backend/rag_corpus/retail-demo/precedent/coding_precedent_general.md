# Retail — Historical Coded-Invoice Precedent

Version: 2.0.0
Effective from: 2026-01-01

Prior invoices coded and approved by a human, retained as precedent. When a new
non-PO invoice resembles one of these, the precedent is a valid citation
alongside or instead of the policy clause. Precedent is how the system learns a
specific vendor's billing language maps to an account without re-deriving it
every time. Each entry names the vendor, the kind of line it bills, and the
account a human confirmed.

## Initech Services — contract engineering

Initech Services (vendor V-1004) bills monthly for contract engineering and
staff-augmentation support. These invoices have consistently been coded to
account 6500 (Professional Services). A new Initech invoice for "contract
engineering support", "engineering retainer", or "staff augmentation" follows
this precedent: 6500.

## Analytics platform renewals

Annual and monthly analytics-platform and data-platform licence renewals have
been coded to 7200 (Software Subscriptions). A "platform licence renewal",
"analytics subscription", or "data platform annual" invoice follows this
precedent: 7200.

## Trade-show and event production

Trade-show booth production and event build-out invoices have been coded to
6600 (Marketing and Advertising), even when billed by a construction or
fabrication contractor, because the spend is promotional. A "booth production",
"event build", or "exhibition stand" invoice follows this precedent: 6600.

## Cloud hosting — infrastructure

Monthly cloud-hosting and infrastructure invoices (compute, storage,
bandwidth) from an IaaS provider have been coded to 7200 (Software
Subscriptions). A "cloud hosting", "compute usage", or "storage and egress"
invoice follows this precedent: 7200.

## Law firm — retainer and matters

Outside-counsel invoices for legal advice and specific matters have been coded
to 6500 (Professional Services). A "legal fees", "matter #", or "counsel
retainer" invoice follows this precedent: 6500.

## Recruitment agency — placement fees

Placement and contingency fees from recruitment agencies for filling roles have
been coded to 6500 (Professional Services). A "placement fee", "candidate
placement", or "recruitment retainer" invoice follows this precedent: 6500.

## Office pantry and janitorial supplies

Recurring office pantry (coffee, water, snacks) and janitorial-consumable
invoices have been coded to 6410 (Office Supplies). A "pantry restock",
"janitorial supplies", or "breakroom consumables" invoice follows this
precedent: 6410.

## Peripheral hardware — docking stations and monitors

Invoices for docking stations, external monitors, keyboards, and headsets below
the capitalisation threshold have been coded to 6420 (Computer Supplies). A
"docking stations", "external monitor", or "peripheral kit" invoice follows
this precedent: 6420, not 7200.

## Digital media buy — advertising

Paid digital advertising and media-buy invoices (search, social, programmatic)
have been coded to 6600 (Marketing and Advertising). A "media buy", "paid
search", or "programmatic advertising" invoice follows this precedent: 6600.

## Audit and tax preparation

Annual external audit fees and tax-return preparation fees from an accounting
firm have been coded to 6500 (Professional Services). An "audit fee", "tax
preparation", or "year-end audit" invoice follows this precedent: 6500.
