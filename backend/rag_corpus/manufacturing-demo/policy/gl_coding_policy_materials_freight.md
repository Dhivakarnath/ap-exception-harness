# Manufacturing — Coding Guide: Materials and Freight (Accounts 5000 and 5100)

Version: 1.1.0
Effective from: 2026-01-01

This guide covers the two goods-related accounts that dominate this tenant:
5000 (Raw Materials) and 5100 (Freight In). Because the tenant is
overwhelmingly PO-backed, every non-PO materials or freight invoice deserves a
missing-PO check before coding. Each clause resolves to 5000 or 5100 unless it
redirects.

## 1. Metals and structural materials

Account 5000 — Raw Materials.

Steel, aluminium, sheet, bar, tube, plate, and structural stock are coded to
5000. On a non-PO metals invoice, confirm there is no missing PO first.

## 2. Polymers, resins, and compounds

Account 5000 — Raw Materials.

Polymers, resins, compounds, and moulding materials consumed in production are
coded to 5000.

## 3. Components and fasteners

Account 5000 — Raw Materials.

Fasteners, bearings, gaskets, seals, castings, and purchased components that
become part of the finished product are coded to 5000.

## 4. Production consumables

Account 5000 — Raw Materials.

Welding wire, cutting fluid, abrasives, machining lubricant, and mould-release
agents used up in production are coded to 5000 as materials of production, not
as office supplies (which this tenant does not code).

## 5. Inbound freight and haulage

Account 5100 — Freight In.

Inbound freight, haulage, carriage-in, and delivery on purchased materials are
coded to 5100, separate from the materials. Fuel surcharges and accessorials on
an inbound shipment are 5100.

## 6. Demurrage and detention

Account 5100 — Freight In.

Demurrage and detention charges on inbound containers or trailers are coded to
5100 as part of inbound freight cost.

## 7. Bundled materials and freight

Split — 5000 for materials, 5100 for freight.

When one invoice lists materials and their inbound freight separately, split the
materials to 5000 and the freight to 5100, citing each. Do not fold freight into
the materials line.

## 8. What does not belong here

Contracted labour and engineering are 6500, not 5000 or 5100. Outbound customer
shipping is out of scope and routes to a human. Office, computer, software, and
marketing spend have no account for this tenant. When a non-PO goods invoice is
unclear, check for a missing PO and, if still unclear, abstain.
