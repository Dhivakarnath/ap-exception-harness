# Manufacturing — Chart of Accounts Guide for Expense Coding

Version: 1.1.0
Effective from: 2026-01-01

This guide defines the three codeable accounts for the manufacturing tenant:
5000 Raw Materials, 5100 Freight In, and 6500 Professional Services. Unlike the
retail tenant, this tenant does not code office, computer, software, or
marketing spend — such items route to a human. The guide exists so that the
minority of non-PO invoices are coded consistently and never forced into a
retail account that does not apply here.

Account 2000 (Accounts Payable) is a control account and is never a coding
target.

## Account 5000 — Raw Materials

Direct materials and production consumables that enter or are used up making the
product. Include: metals, polymers, resins, castings, fasteners, bearings,
gaskets, seals, welding wire, cutting fluid, abrasives, and mould-release
agents. Exclude: freight to move them (5100) and contracted labour (6500). The
account most confused with 5000 is 5100 — the material is 5000, its inbound
movement cost is 5100. On a non-PO materials invoice, first check for a missing
PO.

## Account 5100 — Freight In

Inbound freight, haulage, and related movement charges on purchased materials.
Include: carriage-in, inbound courier and haulage, fuel surcharges and
accessorials on inbound shipments, and demurrage and detention on inbound
containers or trailers. Exclude: the materials themselves (5000) and outbound
customer shipping (route to a human). When one invoice bundles materials and
freight, split them.

## Account 6500 — Professional Services

Contracted labour, engineering, and technical services delivered to the plant.
Include: contracted plant maintenance, machine calibration and metrology,
non-destructive testing and inspection, external engineering, equipment-
servicing labour, and refractory work such as a foundry crucible reline.
Exclude: spare parts that become part of the machine (5000) and materials of
production (5000). The account most confused with 5000 is 6500 on a mixed
maintenance invoice — the labour is 6500, the parts are 5000; split them.

## How to choose between two plausible accounts

First, good versus service: a physical material or part is 5000, its movement is
5100, and contracted labour or engineering is 6500. Second, on a maintenance
invoice mixing labour and parts, split — 6500 for labour, 5000 for parts.
Third, if the invoice is office, computer, software, or marketing spend, it has
no account for this tenant and routes to a human. If the account is still
unclear after these tests, abstain and route to a human — an unsure code is
worse than none.
