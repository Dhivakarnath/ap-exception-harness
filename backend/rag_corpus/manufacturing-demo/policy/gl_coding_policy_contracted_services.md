# Manufacturing — Coding Guide: Contracted Services (Account 6500)

Version: 1.1.0
Effective from: 2026-01-01

This guide covers contracted labour, engineering, and technical services coded
to account 6500 (Professional Services), the manufacturing tenant's main
non-materials account. The recurring hazard is the mixed maintenance invoice
that bundles labour (6500) and parts (5000). Each clause resolves to 6500 unless
it redirects.

## 1. Contracted plant maintenance

Account 6500 — Professional Services.

Contracted plant and facility maintenance labour is coded to 6500. Any spare
parts the contractor separately bills that become part of the machine are
materials coded to 5000; split a mixed invoice.

## 2. Machine calibration and metrology

Account 6500 — Professional Services.

Third-party calibration, metrology, and gauge-verification services are coded to
6500.

## 3. Inspection and non-destructive testing

Account 6500 — Professional Services.

Non-destructive testing, weld inspection, pressure testing, and quality
inspection services are coded to 6500.

## 4. External engineering services

Account 6500 — Professional Services.

External engineering design, process engineering, and technical consulting for
the plant are coded to 6500.

## 5. Refractory and specialist trades

Account 6500 — Professional Services.

Refractory work such as a foundry crucible reline, furnace relining, and
specialist trade labour are coded to 6500. Refractory *materials* consumed, if
separately billed, are 5000.

## 6. Equipment servicing and repair labour

Account 6500 — Professional Services.

Equipment-servicing and repair labour from an external provider is coded to
6500. As with maintenance, separately billed replacement parts are 5000.

## 7. Mixed labour-and-parts invoices

Split — 6500 for labour, 5000 for parts.

A maintenance or repair invoice that bundles service labour and replacement
parts is split: the labour to 6500 and the parts to 5000, citing each. Do not
lump the whole invoice into one account.

## 8. What does not belong in 6500

Materials and consumables of production are 5000. Inbound freight is 5100.
Office, computer, software, and marketing services have no account for this
tenant and route to a human. When a services invoice is unclear, abstain.
