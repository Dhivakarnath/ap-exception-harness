# Manufacturing — Coded-Invoice Precedent: Materials and Freight

Version: 1.1.0
Effective from: 2026-01-01

Prior materials and freight invoices coded and approved by a human, retained as
precedent for accounts 5000 (Raw Materials) and 5100 (Freight In). Each records
the vendor, the billing language, and the confirmed account.

## SteelWorks — structural steel

SteelWorks bills for structural steel and plate. On the rare non-PO occasion,
after confirming no missing PO, these code to 5000 (Raw Materials). A "structural
steel", "sheet and plate", or "bar stock" line follows this precedent: 5000.

## PolyChem — resin and compound

PolyChem bills for moulding resin and compound consumed in production. These
code to 5000. A "moulding resin", "polymer compound", or "masterbatch" line
follows this precedent: 5000.

## FastenCo — fasteners and components

FastenCo bills for fasteners, bearings, and seals that become part of the
product. These code to 5000. A "fasteners", "bearings", or "seals" line follows
this precedent: 5000.

## WeldSupply — welding consumables

WeldSupply bills for welding wire and cutting fluid consumed in production. These
code to 5000 as materials of production, not office supplies. A "welding wire",
"cutting fluid", or "abrasives" line follows this precedent: 5000.

## HeavyHaul — inbound haulage

HeavyHaul bills inbound haulage and carriage on purchased materials. These code
to 5100 (Freight In). An "inbound haulage", "carriage-in", or "delivery charge"
line follows this precedent: 5100.

## HeavyHaul — demurrage

A HeavyHaul invoice for container demurrage on an inbound shipment coded to 5100
(Freight In). A "demurrage" or "detention" line on an inbound shipment follows
this precedent: 5100.

## SteelWorks — materials plus freight (split)

A SteelWorks invoice listing steel and inbound freight separately was split:
steel to 5000 and freight to 5100. A bundled materials-plus-freight invoice
follows this precedent: split, materials 5000, freight 5100.
