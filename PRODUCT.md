# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Accounts-payable operations leads, reviewers, controllers, and implementation
engineers who need to process invoice exceptions and verify every automated
decision during daily operational work.

## Product Purpose

Process invoices through extraction, deterministic policy checks, grounded GL
coding, routing, and human review while making each decision inspectable and
auditable. Success means operators can trust the result, identify failures
quickly, and distinguish measured evidence from unavailable or illustrative
data.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, and accountable. The product should communicate expert
confidence without appearing decorative, experimental, or over-automated.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, decorative metric walls, opaque AI confidence
scores, gratuitous gradients or motion, and any interface that presents
synthetic or pending data as measured production evidence.

## Design Principles

1. Show evidence, not claims.
2. Keep the primary operational task visible and remove non-actionable metrics.
3. Make live and historical states unmistakable.
4. Preserve auditability from source document through final decision.
5. Prefer a small set of meaningful controls and measurements over feature breadth.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Do not rely on color alone for status, preserve visible
keyboard focus, support reduced motion, maintain readable contrast in both
themes, and keep dense operational data usable at narrow viewport widths.
